1. End-to-End System Architecture & Ingestion Pipeline
Production Spec

The Barclays Intelligent Email Triage platform operates as a high-throughput, low-latency defense layer specifically calibrated for user-reported suspicious emails. It solves a focused operational challenge: separating high-volume nuisance Spam from genuine Phishing attacks, while deferring uncertain or borderline cases to Tier-2 SOC analysts.

ARCHITECTURE DIAGRAM 1.1 RFC-822 Ingestion → Feature Pipeline → Confidence Routing → SOC Feedback Loop
Core Architectural Principle: Model-Agnostic Adapter Contract
Every triage execution passes through a unified Python Protocol: Predictor.triage_eml(raw_bytes, model_choice) or Predictor.triage_json(json_dict, model_choice). The API, feature extraction engine, SQLite feedback store, and HTML frontend remain strictly decoupled from the internal mechanics of whether LightGBM or RoBERTa produces the output vector.

3. Trust Score Equation & Multi-Tier SOC Routing
Dual-Signal Stack

Raw model class probabilities are prone to overconfidence when presented with subtle social engineering. The system computes a composite Trust Score (0–100%) combining top probability and classification margin before executing any automated action.

Trust Score Mathematical Definition src/inference/threshold_router.py
Margin = | P(Phishing) - P(Spam) |
Trust Score = ( 0.60 × Max_Probability + 0.40 × Margin ) × 100

6. Calibration Deep Dive & Expected Calibration Error (ECE)
Engineering Post-Mortem

In high-stakes cybersecurity triage, a model's stated confidence must ideally reflect empirical ground truth. A model predicting 90% confidence should be accurate in 9 out of 10 instances.

Why ECE Failed to Reach < 0.05

Across all three models (Logistic Regression, LightGBM, RoBERTa), ECE hovered between 0.39 and 0.45. This is not an architectural defect, but a property of the underlying textual dataset: spam and phishing vocabulary in public corpora are hyper-separable. Trees and embeddings immediately push predictions into extreme >0.99 or <0.01 bands.

Why Post-Hoc Scaling Can't Fix It Alone

Platt scaling and Temperature scaling (optimal T=1.14) divide log-odds. When 94% of dataset predictions have logit magnitudes exceeding ±8.0, temperature scaling cannot artificially smear predictions into smooth mid-range bins without degrading phishing detection recall.

The Operational Solution: Routing Bands Instead of Raw Probabilities
Because discrimination (ROC-AUC 0.9845) is near flawless, the operational triage engine does not rely on raw probabilities for risk math. It utilizes the composite Trust Score (0.6 MaxP + 0.4 Margin) to segregate the 3.2% of borderline cases directly into the Tier-2 Analyst Queue.
7. Attack Taxonomy & Active SOC Threat Profiles
5 Threat Vectors

The triage platform is explicitly trained and verified against real attack types encountered in banking and corporate enterprise environments:

1. Credential Harvesting Phishing Critical Threat

Simulates corporate SSO, Microsoft 365, Barclays internal portals, or financial institutions. Leverages urgent account suspension threats and typo-squatted domains leading to fake reverse-proxy credential interception portals.

2. Business Email Compromise (BEC) / Wire Fraud High Severity

Pure social engineering. Usually contains zero links and zero attachments. Impersonates C-suite executives (CEO, CFO, Group Treasurer) instructing finance personnel to execute emergency wire releases or alter supplier bank details.

3. Borderline Package / Account Delivery Notices Ambiguous • Review Queue

Ambiguous delivery alerts (DHL, DPD, Royal Mail) with generic tracking links. Possesses low classification margin (margin < 0.20), routing to human Tier-2 analysts for payload and header verification.

4. Unsolicited Commercial Bulk Spam Nuisance • Auto-Suppress

Bulk marketing campaigns, B2B lead generation, unsolicited sales pitches, and newsletter blasts. High link count, promotional language, but no credential theft or financial wire fraud intent.

9. SOC Feedback Loop & Continuous Retraining Studio
Closed-Loop Governance

Every email triaged through the system is durably recorded in data/feedback.db. When Tier-2 analysts review borderline tickets in /app/queue, their resolutions generate labeled ground truth.

1. Analyst Audit Trail

Analyst agreements and overrides are tracked with timestamps, analyst notes, and original feature attributions. Disagreements automatically update rolling drift metrics.

2. Zero-Downtime Hot Reload

When newly trained models pass the hard validation gate (Recall ≥ 98.0%), the system promotes the checkpoint and reloads the inference engine in memory via /api/model/reload with zero server restart or dropped requests.
